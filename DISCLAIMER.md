# Legal and acceptable-use notice

VRKA is a general-purpose local media-download interface.

Users are responsible for:

- complying with copyright law;
- complying with website terms;
- obtaining permission to save media;
- respecting privacy and access controls;
- choosing lawful advanced command options;
- securing their own cookies and account data.
